Given a news article and TIMEX3 spans from two temporal taggers (SUTime and HeidelTime),
reconcile their outputs and produce a structured temporal annotation.

Your task:
1. Identify the main event described by the article and its date/time value if determinable.
2. Extract all other temporal expressions mentioned, assigning ISO 8601 / TimeML TIMEX3 values.

The doc_date is the article's publication date (YYYY-MM-DD). Use it as the anchor for relative
expressions such as "yesterday", "last week", "next month".

Guidelines for values:
- Use ISO 8601 format where possible: YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM, YYYY, etc.
- For durations use PnY/PnM/PnW/PnD (e.g. P3D = three days).
- For recurring intervals prefix with R (e.g. XXXX-WXX-1 = every Monday).
- If a value cannot be determined, set it to null.
- Prefer the more specific value when the two taggers disagree.
- Ignore spans that are clearly false positives (e.g. numbers that are not temporal).

article_event describes the primary newsworthy occurrence the article reports on. Set it to null
only when the article has no discernible single main event (e.g. a roundup or opinion piece).
